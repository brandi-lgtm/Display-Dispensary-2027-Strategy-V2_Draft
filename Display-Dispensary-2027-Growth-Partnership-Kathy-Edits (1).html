The Direction

A More Strategic Approach

The goal for 2027 is to move beyond a fixed annual media schedule and build a program designed to respond to performance, sales feedback, and changing priorities throughout the year.

The partnership is structured around ongoing collaboration, allowing mg and Display Dispensary to identify what is generating the strongest response and adjust the program accordingly.

Demand Generation

Maintain consistent exposure across mg’s editorial, advertising, and digital platforms.

Creative + Conversion Optimization

Test messaging, calls to action, creative concepts, and campaign approaches to improve response.

Lead Generation

Create pathways from marketing activity into qualified inquiries, which can be handed directly to the Display Dispensary sales team.

Measurement + Optimization

Evaluate performance and adjust priorities based on what is generating the strongest engagement and sales opportunities.

How We Work Together

Built to Evolve

The program is designed to remain flexible throughout the year. As priorities change, creative evolves, or certain programs generate a stronger response, the focus can shift rather than remain tied to a plan established at the beginning of the year.

The goal is to continually evaluate what is creating real interest, what is generating the strongest response, and where the investment is having the greatest impact.
